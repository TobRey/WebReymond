About us

Thai cooking
means fresh cooking

Iang’s Thai Food is a takeaway on Hauptstrasse in Bubendorf.

What matters to us

Fresh, honest, Thai

We cook Thai food the way we learned it: fresh ingredients, prepared on the spot, no shortcuts. It takes a few minutes longer — and you can taste it.

You pick your food up and take it with you. That keeps things small and leaves the kitchen free for what counts: the cooking.
