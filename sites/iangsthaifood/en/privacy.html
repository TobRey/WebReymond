Privacy policy

What this website does with your data — and what it does not do.

In short

This website works without tools from Google, Meta or anyone else. No fonts, maps or videos are loaded from outside, and no profile is built about you. That is why there is no consent banner to click away here.

We follow the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Controller

Iangs Thai Food, Hauptstrasse 142, 4416 Bubendorf, Switzerland. Phone 076 442 70 44. Email: to be added: email address of the business.

If you write to us through the contact form

You send us your name, your email address, optionally your phone number and the text of your message. We use these details solely to answer your enquiry.

To keep the form from being abused by machines, the server checks how quickly it was submitted and limits the number of messages. For that, a non-reversible hash of your IP address is stored for a short time — not the IP address itself.

Your message is delivered by email and kept only as long as handling it requires. It is not passed on.

Visitor counting

We count how often the individual pages are opened. All that is stored is which page was visited how often on which day.

  • No personal data is stored.
  • No IP addresses are stored.
  • No cookies are set and no lasting identifiers are handed out.
  • There is no sharing with third parties.
  • No analytics tool from Google, Meta or any other provider is used.

These figures cannot be traced back to who visited the website. A summary of the figures goes to the technical support of the website once a week.

Server and hosting

This website is hosted with a provider: to be added: name and location of the hosting provider. As with any web hosting, the provider may log technical access data such as the address called up, the time and the IP address. These logs serve secure operation and are deleted according to the provider's retention periods. We do not evaluate them.

Cookies

The public pages of this website set no cookies.

Only the protected support area, used by the business itself, sets two technically necessary cookies: one so the access code does not have to be entered on every visit, and one so the conversation can be found again. Neither contains personal data in plain text.

Your rights

You may ask at any time whether and which data we process about you, and you may request that it be corrected or deleted. Just get in touch — the details are in the imprint.

Changes

If something changes on this website that affects data protection, we will update this policy.
