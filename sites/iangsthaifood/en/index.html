The takeaway

A takeaway on Hauptstrasse

Iang’s Thai Food is a small takeaway in Bubendorf. Over lunchtime the kitchen is yours: tell us what you would like, we prepare it fresh, and you take it with you.

No big dining room, no long waits — Thai food to take away, and that is all it needs to be.
