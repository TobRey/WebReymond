2. Principle

Personal data is processed only as far as necessary to operate the website and to answer your enquiries. The basis is the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and its ordinance. No data is sold and no profiles are built.

3. Server logs

When the website is called up, the server necessarily receives details such as the IP address, date and time, the address requested, the amount of data transferred, the browser and the operating system. These logs are kept by the hosting provider, serve secure operation and troubleshooting, and are not combined with other data.

These logs are kept by the web hosting provider that delivers the website on my behalf, and are deleted there automatically after a short time. I am happy to name the provider on request — a short email is enough.

4. Contact form and email

If you use the contact form, your name, email address, subject and message are transmitted and delivered as an email to tobias.reymond05@gmail.com. These details are used exclusively to answer your enquiry and are not passed on to third parties.

To guard against automated junk mail, the server checks an extra field invisible to humans, the time between opening and submitting the form, and the number of messages per email address. For this, the sender address is stored in a shortened, non-reversible form (a hash value) for 24 hours. No captcha and no third-party service is used.

Messages are sent through the hosting provider's mail server; delivery to the mailbox named above is handled by Google Ireland Ltd. or Google LLC, since it is a Gmail address. If you prefer to avoid that, please contact me by another route.

5. Visitor counting

To see which content is being read, this website counts page views itself — without any third-party service. What is stored is only: the date, the address requested within this website, and a counter. IP addresses are not stored, neither truncated nor encrypted. No recognition token is set that would allow individuals to be followed across several visits.

6. Cookies and local storage

The public part of this website sets no cookies. So that a single visit is not counted more than once, one value is stored in the browser's session storage (sessionStorage). That value contains no personal data, never leaves your browser and is deleted when the tab is closed.

In the protected editing area at /admin, which is open to the operator only, a technically necessary session cookie is set. Without it, signing in is not possible. It has no relevance for visitors to the public pages.

7. Third-party services

No fonts, map services, video embeds, analytics tools or ad networks are loaded from external servers. All files are stored on this website's own server.

The page contains links to a LinkedIn profile and to the website dj-atze.com. Both are ordinary links: a connection to the respective provider is only established once you click them, and from that point that provider's privacy policy applies. As long as you do not click, nothing is transmitted.

8. Retention

Enquiries by form or email
For as long as necessary to deal with them, then deleted — unless statutory retention duties apply.
Counter values
As totals per day and page; they contain no personal reference in the first place.
Hash values of the spam brake
24 hours, then removed automatically.
Server logs
According to the hosting provider's periods.

9. Security

The website is delivered encrypted (HTTPS). The editing area is protected by a password that is only stored as an Argon2id hash; it is nowhere kept in clear text. Failed attempts are rate limited. Despite all care, complete security cannot be guaranteed when data is transmitted over the internet.

10. Your rights

You have the right to information about the data processed about you and to have it corrected or deleted. You may also object to processing and — where applicable — request that your data be handed over. A short message to tobias.reymond05@gmail.com is enough. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

11. Changes

This policy may be adjusted if the website or the legal situation changes. The version published here is the one that applies.
